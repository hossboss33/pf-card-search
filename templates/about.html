About this index

What it covers, honestly

This index covers Public Forum evidence disclosed on openCaselist, which is most of the national-circuit corpus from roughly the mid-2010s onward. It is not every card ever cut. Local-circuit teams that do not disclose are invisible to this tool, as they are to any tool built on the wiki. Pre-openCaselist archives are out of scope for this version. Precision about coverage is a feature.

Credits

This project exists because other people did the hard part.

  • The Yusuf5/OpenCaselist research dataset (MIT license) supplies the bulk historical corpus, parsed from the .docx files uploaded to openCaselist.
  • openCaselist, the community-run disclosure wiki maintained by Ashtar Communications, is the source of everything here. It is open source at ashtarcommunications/caselist.
  • The Verbatim documentation defines the speech-document conventions the parser follows.

Data and licenses

Card bodies are excerpts from copyrighted articles, papers, and books, redistributed inside the debate community by established norm. This deployment is private by default and binds to localhost. Debater names arrive truncated to two characters in the research dataset and are kept that way. Cites are displayed and exported exactly as the original cutter disclosed them, never restamped.
